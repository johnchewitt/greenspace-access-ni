# Greenspace Access NI — Demo Script

A ~12-minute walkthrough for presenting to Outscape and stakeholders. Each step names
what to click, what to say, and the caveat to keep handy. The app lands on **Overview**.

## Before the demo (2 minutes)

- Open the live app in a fresh tab: <https://johnchewitt.github.io/greenspace-access-ni/>
  (don't refresh mid-demo; the first-visit tour may appear — Skip it, or use it as your opener).
- Glance that the Overview hero shows live numbers (hectares, % within 400 m). If any show
  "—", the ORNI services are momentarily unreachable — the app says so honestly; the
  published survey content is unaffected.
- Have one council in mind for the deep-dive (Belfast demos best; Fermanagh & Omagh for
  the rural contrast).

## 1 · Overview — the pitch (1 min)

> "Better Greenspace. Better Places. Better Lives. This app puts Outscape's two evidence
> bases in one place: the Greenspace NI Map — what's physically there — and the proposed
> Greenspace Standards Index — how residents experience it. Everything on screen is a
> published value or a live query of our public services; nothing is invented."

Point at the four hero numbers: the published 77% overall and 65% access-to beside the
live hectares and the live "% within a 400 m walk". Two kinds of evidence, one story.

## 2 · Explore — the index on the map (2 min)

Click **Explore the map →**.

- Switch theme **Overall → Access To**: the map's weak spot appears in the west.
- Toggle **% of maximum ↔ Index (NI = 100)** — both are published formats.
- Click **Belfast**: the profile panel shows 79.7%, index 103.4, and its theme chart.
- Flick a Greenspace NI Map layer on (sites/trails) — "this is the estate you already
  publish, consumed live, with status chips when a service is down."

*Caveat:* "Differences between councils are descriptive — margins of error apply."

## 3 · Perception vs Provision — the signature (3 min)

Click **PERCEPTION VS PROVISION** (Belfast still selected).

> "What residents experience, beside what the map shows — geographically synchronised."

- Pan one map; the other follows. The right map carries the real greenspace, trails and
  access points over a streets backdrop.
- Toggle the **household-proximity surface** and switch 400 m → 1 km → 2 km.
- Open the **evidence drawer**: eleven councils in four quadrants. Click **Low / Low** —
  the six councils dim-highlight on both maps. Read the interpretation line aloud; it
  ends with the caveat on purpose: *a prompt for investigation, never a proven gap or a
  causal claim*.

## 4 · Accessibility + Insights — the analyst's tools (2–3 min)

- **ACCESSIBILITY**: the Super Data Zone surface with the 400 m / 1 km / 2 km switch.
  "The published network analysis — we compute no travel times of our own and never fake
  it with circles."
- **INSIGHTS → Dashboard**: live totals (≈87,600 ha, 1,493 sites, 1,385 trails) and NI
  walking-access KPIs, vintage-labelled.
- **INSIGHTS → Opportunities**: the transparent screen — flagged councils with every
  indicator value shown, and the twelve lowest-coverage SDZs queried live. "Flags are
  questions worth asking, not an investment list."

## 5 · Story + Report — the takeaway (2 min)

- **STORY**: arrow through a chapter or two; chapter 6 highlights the three lowest
  Access-To councils on the map.
- Back on **OVERVIEW** (or with a council selected in Explore), click **Create report →
  Save as PDF**: a branded A4-landscape evidence summary with the live map snapshot,
  KPIs, quadrant position and full attribution — ready for a committee paper.

## Questions to put to Outscape (end of demo)

1. Licence year for the NIMA attribution — 2024 or 2026? (A "TBC" marker is held in the docs.)
2. Preferred provision vintage — the app pins the latest (2026) and labels it; happy?
3. Postcode boundaries: results are chart-first because no open-licence BT polygons
   exist — license boundaries, or keep chart-first?
4. Official brand assets: the app uses its own design language pending approved assets.

## If something goes wrong

- **A live figure shows "—" / an offline note** — say: "that number is queried live from
  the public service rather than baked in; it degrades honestly." The survey evidence and
  the map still work: boundaries and all published data are bundled.
- **A layer chip shows an error** — same story; toggle it off and carry on.
- **Projector font looks small** — browser zoom to 110–125% works cleanly at all views.
